---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. Use when the user wants to stress-test their thinking, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview the user relentlessly until you reach a shared understanding. Map this as a design tree: every decision branches into the decisions that hang off it.

Work the tree in rounds. The frontier is every decision whose prerequisites are already settled. Ask the whole frontier in one round, number each question, and give a recommended answer. Then wait.

Facts are the agent's job. Decisions stay with the user. Stop when the frontier is empty and the user confirms a shared understanding. Do not act before that.
